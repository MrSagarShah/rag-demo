# Nimbus — Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Nimbus?

Nimbus is a cloud-based note-taking and knowledge-management app for small teams.
It syncs notes across devices, supports real-time collaboration, and offers
full-text search across everything you've ever written.

## Is there a free trial of Nimbus Pro?

Yes. Every new account gets a 14-day free trial of Nimbus Pro with no credit card
required. At the end of the trial you are automatically moved to the free plan
unless you choose to subscribe. You can start the trial from the Billing page in
Settings.

## What is included in the free plan?

The free plan includes up to 3 notebooks, 1 GB of storage, and single-device sync.
Real-time collaboration, unlimited notebooks, and version history are Pro features.

## Which platforms are supported?

Nimbus runs on the web, macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android. All clients share the
same account and sync automatically.

## How do I contact support?

Email support@nimbus.example or use the in-app chat. Pro subscribers receive
priority responses within one business day.
