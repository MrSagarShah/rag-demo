# Returns Policy

## Return window

You may return most items within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund. The
30-day clock starts on the date the carrier marks your order as delivered.

## Condition requirements

Returned items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all
accessories and documentation. Items showing signs of use may be subject to a
restocking fee of up to 15%.

## How to start a return

1. Open the Orders page in your account.
2. Select the order and click **Return item**.
3. Print the prepaid return label and drop the package at any partner carrier location.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after
we receive and inspect the returned item.

## Non-returnable items

Gift cards, downloadable software, and personalized items are final sale and
cannot be returned. Opened consumable goods (such as ink or batteries) are also
non-returnable for safety reasons.

## Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. To swap an item, return the original for a
refund and place a new order.
