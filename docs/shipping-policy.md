# Shipping Policy

## Processing time

Orders are processed within 1 business day. Orders placed after 2 PM local time,
or on weekends and holidays, begin processing the next business day.

## Shipping methods and delivery times

- **Standard shipping**: 3–5 business days. Free on orders over $50, otherwise $5.99.
- **Expedited shipping**: 2 business days. $14.99.
- **Overnight shipping**: next business day if ordered before 2 PM. $29.99.

Delivery times are estimates from the ship date and do not include the processing
time above.

## International shipping

We ship to over 40 countries. International delivery typically takes 7–14 business
days. Customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes charged by
the destination country.

## Tracking

A tracking number is emailed as soon as your order ships. You can also view live
tracking from the Orders page in your account.

## Lost or delayed packages

If tracking shows no movement for 7 business days, contact support and we will open
a carrier investigation or reship your order at no charge.
